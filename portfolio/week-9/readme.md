# DACA Week 9: Career Preparation — From Data Projects to Professional Impact

#### Business Problem
After eight weeks of intensive work on the UrbanStyle project, the challenge shifts from technical execution to professional communication. Operations Manager Liis Koppel needs to hire new analysts but lacks the framework to identify top talent. This project focuses on translating 1,247+ sales records, automated pipelines, and interactive dashboards into a professional brand (CV, LinkedIn, and Portfolio) that clearly demonstrates business value to employers.

#### Approach
The project utilized a strategic "Market-Ready" workflow to transition from a learning environment to the professional job market:
*   **CV Optimization:** Transformed the UrbanStyle experience from a list of tasks into a marketing document focused on quantified results using action verbs.
*   **STAR Method:** Structured behavioral and technical interview responses using the Situation-Task-Action-Result framework to provide concrete evidence of problem-solving skills.
*   **LinkedIn SEO:** Optimized the professional profile with targeted keywords (SQL, Python, Power BI) and a narrative-driven "About" section to increase visibility to recruiters.
*   **Employer Perspective:** Conducted a "Recruitment Guide" exercise, acting as a hiring manager to identify the "Green Flags" in data analyst portfolios and technical documentation.

#### Key Findings
*   **Quantified Impact:** Identified that the UrbanStyle project processed over **10,000 sales records**, revealing a VIP segment worth **€127,000**.
*   **Efficiency Gains:** The automated RFM pipeline saved approximately **19 hours of annual labor**, a critical metric for operational efficiency.
*   **Conversion Success:** Data-driven marketing strategies derived from the analysis demonstrated a potential **23% increase in conversion rates**.
*   **Market Readiness:** Mapped current skills against the Estonian DA market requirements, confirming readiness for roles ranging from **€28,000 to €42,000** annually.

#### Technical Stack
*   **Documentation:** GitHub for portfolio hosting and Markdown for technical READMEs.
*   **Networking:** LinkedIn for professional visibility and market engagement.
*   **Core Tools:** Continued use of SQL (PostgreSQL), Python (Pandas), and Power BI to demonstrate technical proficiency in the portfolio.

#### How to Run
1.  **Audit:** Perform a final review of all Week 1-8 project READMEs to ensure they include "Business Problem" and "Key Findings."
2.  **CV Construction:** Use the "Action Verb + Number + Result" formula to update your experience section.
3.  **LinkedIn Update:** Apply the [Role] | [Key Skills] | [Value Proposition] formula to your headline.
4.  **Portfolio Walk-through:** Record or practice a 2-minute "PechaKucha" style presentation of your strongest project (e.g., RFM Automation).
5.  **Networking:** Identify 5 target companies and send personalized connection requests to their data or hiring managers.

#### Lessons Learned
*   **Numbers Talk:** Learned that saying "I know SQL" is weak; saying "I used SQL to identify €127k in revenue" is what closes the deal.
*   **Background as an Asset:** Realized that a career-changer's previous experience (e.g., in finance, marketing, or teaching) is a unique competitive advantage, not a drawback.
*   **The ATS Filter:** Understood the importance of keywords and standard formatting to pass through Applicant Tracking Systems (ATS) used by 70% of large companies.

#### AI Usage
AI was utilized as a "Career Coach" to refine STAR interview responses, optimize the LinkedIn "About" section for SEO, and tailor the CV summary to match specific junior data analyst job descriptions.
